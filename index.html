The #1 Winter Heating Mistake Costing Homeowners Hundreds — And the 60-Second Fix Going Viral

Stop paying to heat empty rooms and leaky air ducts. See how this compact NASA-inspired ceramic heater warms the exact space you’re in.
by Andrea Stewart ✔ Verified Home Editor • November 20, 2026
Winter Outlook Note: With colder weather setting in and energy rates staying elevated, many households are looking for a faster, more targeted way to stay warm without heating empty rooms all day.

If you’ve ever waited forever for a central system to warm up your room — only to see a steep heating bill at the end of the month — you already know how inefficient traditional heating can feel.

Standard central heating pushes warm air through duct networks — where up to 40% of your heat can be lost along the way before it ever reaches the spot where you’re sitting or sleeping.

Why Heating Every Empty Room Rarely Makes Sense

Most of the time, you’re spending hours in just one or two spaces — your bedroom at night, your home office during the day, or the living room in the evening. Warming the entire house at full blast wastes energy on rooms nobody is using.

"A compact PTC ceramic convection heater lets you bring steady, comfortable warmth directly to the room you’re in — in about 60 seconds, with no ductwork or complicated setup."

That practical approach is why the Hotjak Portable Heater has been catching so much attention this season.

NASA-Inspired PTC Ceramic Heating Explained

Space missions like NASA’s "New Horizons" spacecraft — which traveled into deep cold where temperatures dropped to -80°C — relied on PTC (Positive Temperature Coefficient) heating technology known for rapid thermal response, high efficiency, and compact packaging.

The Hotjak Portable Heater brings PTC ceramic heating into an everyday plug-in unit designed to start warming up in 60 seconds, with built-in overheat protection and quiet operation.
